# Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Google Ads?

Google Ads is Google's paid advertising platform that allows businesses
to promote their products and services across Google's advertising
network.

### Do you manage existing Google Ads accounts?

Yes. We can audit, restructure and manage existing campaigns as well as
build new accounts.

### Do you provide Google Ads setup?

Yes. Campaign setup, keyword research, ad creation, targeting and basic
tracking can be included depending on the package.

### Is the Google Ads budget included in your pricing?

No. Our management fee and your advertising spend are separate.

The advertising budget is paid directly to Google.

### How much should I spend on Google Ads?

There is no single budget that works for every business. The appropriate
budget depends on your industry, competition, target locations,
keywords, conversion value and business objectives.

We can recommend a starting range after reviewing your business and
market.

### Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No. We do not guarantee a specific number of leads or sales because
campaign results depend on factors including market demand, competition,
offer, landing page, pricing and conversion behaviour.

### Do you run Search Ads?

Yes. Search Ads are one of our core Google Ads services.

### Do you manage Google Shopping?

Yes. Shopping campaign management and Merchant Center support can be
included depending on your package.

### Do you manage Performance Max campaigns?

Yes. Performance Max can be used where it makes sense for the business
and campaign objectives.

### Do you provide remarketing?

Yes. We can set up and manage remarketing campaigns based on available
audience data and campaign requirements.

### Can you target specific cities?

Yes. Location targeting can be configured around your target service
areas.

### Do you set up conversion tracking?

Yes. Conversion tracking setup or review can be included depending on
your package and technical requirements.

### Can you manage YouTube Ads?

Yes. YouTube advertising can be included in larger or customized Google
Ads campaigns.

### How often do you optimize campaigns?

Optimization frequency depends on the selected package, account size and
campaign activity. Ongoing management includes regular performance
review and optimization.

### Do you provide monthly reports?

Yes. Monthly reporting is included in our ongoing management packages.

### Can you manage international campaigns?

Yes. Campaigns can be structured for local, national or international
markets depending on your business requirements.

### Do you work with international clients?

Yes. SKY Tech works with businesses in Pakistan, the USA, the UK and
beyond.
