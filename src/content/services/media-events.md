# Frequently Asked Questions

### What types of events do you cover?

We cover corporate events, conferences, seminars, workshops, product
launches, exhibitions, networking events, award ceremonies and other
business or organizational events.

### Do you provide both photography and videography?

Yes. Photography and videography can be booked separately or combined
into an event coverage package.

### Do you manage events as well as cover them?

Yes. We can provide event planning and production support depending on
the selected package.

### Do you provide live streaming?

Yes. Live streaming is available for eligible events and can be included
as an add-on or custom event package.

### Can you cover events for social media?

Yes. We can create photographs, Stories, Reels, short videos and other
content during the event.

### Can you provide same-day content?

Yes. Same-day or rapid-turnaround content can be arranged for selected
events.

### Do you provide event branding?

Yes. We can support event branding including backdrops, signage,
presentation graphics and other visual materials.

### Can you cover a full-day event?

Yes. Full-day coverage is available through our Premium or Custom Event
packages.

### Do you provide edited photographs?

Yes. Final photographs are professionally selected and edited according
to the agreed package.

### Do you provide highlight videos?

Yes. Highlight videos can be included in packages or ordered separately.

### Can you interview speakers or guests?

Yes. Speaker, guest, customer and team interviews can be arranged as
part of the media coverage.

### Can event footage be used for social media?

Yes. We can deliver content specifically formatted for social platforms,
subject to the agreed deliverables.

### Do you provide raw footage?

Raw footage can be provided where agreed as part of the project. Storage
and transfer requirements may apply.

### Do you provide drone coverage?

Drone coverage may be available depending on location, venue, weather,
permissions and applicable requirements.

### Do you travel for events?

Yes. Travel can be arranged for events outside our normal service area.
Additional travel costs may apply.

### Do you guarantee specific event shots?

We create a coverage plan around your requirements, but event
photography and videography involve live environments and cannot
guarantee every spontaneous moment.

### How long does post-event editing take?

Delivery time depends on the volume and complexity of the content.
Standard event photographs may take several business days, while
highlight films and larger projects may require additional production
time.

### Can you work with our existing event team?

Yes. We can coordinate with your event planner, venue, AV team,
marketing team or other vendors.

### Do you work with international clients?

Yes. SKY Tech works with businesses and organizations in Pakistan, the
USA, the UK and beyond.
