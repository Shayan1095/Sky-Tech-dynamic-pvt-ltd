# Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a domain?

A domain is the web address people use to access your website, such as
**yourbusiness.com**.

### What is web hosting?

Web hosting is the server environment where your website files,
databases and related resources are stored and made accessible online.

### Do I need both a domain and hosting?

For a typical website, yes. The domain is your website's address, while
hosting provides the infrastructure where your website lives.

### Do you provide domain registration?

Yes. We can assist with domain registration and configuration for
available domain extensions.

### Can you transfer my existing domain?

Yes. We can assist with domain transfers and DNS configuration.

### Can you migrate my existing website?

Yes. Website migration is available for WordPress, business websites and
other supported hosting environments.

### Do you provide WordPress hosting?

Yes. We offer WordPress-focused hosting setup and support.

### Do you provide hosting for e-commerce websites?

Yes. We can configure hosting solutions for WooCommerce and other
e-commerce websites according to their requirements.

### Do you provide SSL?

Yes. We can assist with SSL installation and HTTPS configuration.

### Can you set up business email?

Yes. We can configure professional email addresses using your business
domain.

### Can you fix DNS issues?

Yes. We can troubleshoot and configure common DNS records such as A,
CNAME, MX and TXT records.

### Do you provide website backups?

We can help configure available website and database backup solutions
depending on the hosting environment.

### Will my website be faster with better hosting?

Hosting can affect website performance, but speed also depends on
website code, images, plugins, database configuration, caching and other
factors. We can review the hosting environment and identify potential
improvements.

### Do you guarantee 100% uptime?

No. Actual uptime depends on the hosting infrastructure and provider. We
recommend selecting hosting based on the website's requirements and the
provider's service commitments.

### Are domain and hosting prices recurring?

Yes. Domain registration and hosting generally require renewal according
to their respective billing periods.

### Can I upgrade my hosting later?

In many hosting environments, yes. The appropriate upgrade path depends
on the provider and hosting plan.

### Do you provide ongoing hosting support?

Yes. Ongoing technical support and maintenance can be arranged according
to your requirements.

### Do you work with international clients?

Yes. SKY Tech works with businesses in Pakistan, the USA, the UK and
beyond.
