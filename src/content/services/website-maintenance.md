# Frequently Asked Questions

### What is website maintenance?

Website maintenance is the ongoing process of updating, monitoring,
securing and improving a website to help keep it functional and
reliable.

### Why does my website need maintenance?

Websites use software, plugins, themes, databases and other technologies
that require regular updates and monitoring. Maintenance helps identify
and address issues before they become larger problems.

### Do you maintain WordPress websites?

Yes. WordPress maintenance is one of our core website maintenance
services.

### Do you maintain e-commerce websites?

Yes. We offer dedicated maintenance plans for supported e-commerce
websites.

### Do you update WordPress plugins?

Yes. Plugin and theme updates can be included in WordPress maintenance
packages.

### Will you take backups before updates?

Backup procedures depend on the hosting environment and selected
maintenance setup. We recommend maintaining reliable backups before
significant changes.

### Can you fix my broken website?

Yes. We can investigate and fix many common website issues. Complex
development problems may require a separate quote.

### Can you update my website content?

Yes. Content updates can be included depending on your maintenance
package.

### Can you improve website speed?

Yes. We can audit performance and implement appropriate optimization
where the hosting and website structure allow it.

### Can you remove malware?

We can investigate and provide malware cleanup or recovery services
where technically possible. Severe compromises may require specialized
security work or website restoration.

### Do you provide emergency support?

Emergency support can be provided through selected plans or as a
separate service, subject to availability and issue complexity.

### Will you guarantee my website will never go down?

No. Website uptime depends on hosting infrastructure, software,
third-party services and other factors. Our role is to monitor, maintain
and respond to issues within the agreed scope.

### Do you guarantee that my website will never be hacked?

No. No responsible maintenance provider can guarantee complete immunity
from cyberattacks. Regular updates, backups and security practices can
help reduce risk.

### Can I cancel maintenance anytime?

Maintenance terms depend on the selected plan and agreement. The
cancellation and renewal terms will be provided before the service
begins.

### Do you provide monthly reports?

Yes. Maintenance reports can summarize completed updates, checks, fixes
and recommendations.

### Can you maintain a website developed by another company?

Yes. We can review the website first and, if the technology and access
requirements are suitable, provide ongoing maintenance.

### Do you work with international clients?

Yes. SKY Tech works with businesses in Pakistan, the USA, the UK and
beyond.
