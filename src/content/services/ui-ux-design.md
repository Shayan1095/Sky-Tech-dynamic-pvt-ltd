# Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between UI and UX?

**UX (User Experience)** focuses on how a product works and how users
move through it.

**UI (User Interface)** focuses on how the product looks and how users
interact with its visual interface.

We combine both to create complete digital experiences.

### Do you design websites as well as apps?

Yes. We design websites, mobile applications, dashboards, SaaS products,
portals and other digital interfaces.
